# Frozen validation policy

The independent scorer comparison uses the organizer's published self-evaluation notebook. Confirm perfect, missed, empty, split, merged, and exactly-0.5 cases plus real model outputs. Keep every annotator view associated with its physical image and pool the metric components as the organizer does.

Use grouped development only to calibrate the baseline and decide the challenger. Freeze the model, native probability representation, and export settings before opening confirmation. Hold the Udaipur site aside as a separate check. Assess any potential gain by repeating the same comparison on untouched data. Report real split/merge and unsupported false positives, including thin, faint and near-limb slices where sample support allows.

A candidate needs positive confirmation PQ, an improvement in the mechanism-specific failure mode, and no material growth in its known tradeoff. If mechanism evidence is mixed, select the frozen baseline. Official calibration requires approval for a particular CSV and its SHA-256. Never use the leaderboard to tune an unproven mechanism.
